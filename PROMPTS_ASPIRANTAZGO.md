# Prompts para el arte de la Isla del Aspirantazgo

Para ChatGPT (imágenes), Midjourney, Flux o el generador que uses. Están en inglés porque así salen mejor.

**Cómo usarlos**
1. Pega primero el **bloque de estilo** y, en el mismo mensaje, el prompt de la pieza.
2. Adjunta como referencia tus personajes (trompetista, lectora, nudo). Así el resultado sale con el mismo trazo y el mismo contorno amarillo.
3. Pide **fondo transparente** (PNG).
4. Para lo que va como SVG (íconos, logo), genera la imagen y vectorízala en Illustrator con Calco de imagen.

Ningún prompt pide emblemas oficiales: el C1, el C2 y demás los pones tú encima con tus archivos. Las IAs deforman los logos.

---

## Bloque de estilo (pégalo siempre primero)

```
STYLE: Clean modern cartoon illustration for a children's educational mobile game, matching the attached reference characters exactly: thick black ink outlines, soft cel shading with 2-3 tones, a bright yellow-orange outer outline glow around each character or object, warm saturated colors. Palette anchors: khaki #D9C49A shirts, petroleum green #0E4B45 trousers and skirts, golden yellow #FFC20E scarves, royal blue #1F4FBF, red #D7242F. Friendly, wholesome, Latin American kids aged 10-15. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Transparent background unless a background is requested.
```

## 1. Personajes

**Aspirante (el protagonista, sin pañoleta todavía)**
```
Full-body character turnaround sheet of a 10-year-old Latin American boy, a club applicant: khaki short-sleeve uniform shirt with two chest pockets and NO neckerchief, petroleum green trousers, dark green belt, black shoes. Hopeful, slightly nervous smile. Views: front, 3/4, side, back, plus a T-pose front view for 3D modeling. Same proportions in every view. Neutral light gray background.
```
Haz la misma versión para una niña, cambiando *trousers* por *petroleum green skirt below the knee, white ankle socks, black school shoes* y el peinado por *two long braids*.

**Director de club (el que entrega la pañoleta)**
```
Full-body adult club director, Latin American man in his 30s, white short-sleeve uniform shirt, golden yellow neckerchief with a red border, petroleum green trousers, holding a folded golden yellow neckerchief with both hands as if about to give it. Warm proud smile. Front view and 3/4 view.
```

**Poses de reacción (para los diálogos)**
```
Expression sheet of the same applicant boy from the reference, waist-up, 6 poses in a grid: (1) cheering with both fists up, (2) thinking with hand on chin, (3) surprised with mouth open, (4) giving a thumbs up, (5) reading an open Bible, (6) making a vow: right forearm raised beside his face at 45 degrees, palm forward, four fingers together, thumb folded across the palm. Consistent face and outfit across all poses.
```

## 2. La isla vista desde arriba (portada del mundo o pantalla de carga)

```
Isometric 3D-looking cartoon island floating in a calm turquoise sea at golden hour, for a mobile game world map. On the island: a winding sand path with 10 round stone platforms numbered by small flags, a wooden ceremony stage with a red and gold arch at the center, a tall white flagpole with a plain blue-and-white flag, a crackling campfire with log benches in a circle, three small camping tents (red, blue, green), pine trees around the edges, string lights between trees. A giant golden yellow neckerchief floats and glows above the stage like a trophy. Soft volumetric light, gentle clouds, cozy and epic. Square composition, no text.
```
